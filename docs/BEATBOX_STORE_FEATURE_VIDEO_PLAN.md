# BeatBox 16:9 Store-Feature Video Production Plan

## Goal

Create a polished **30-second, 16:9 landscape BeatBox feature video** for app-store listing use, with a reusable master suitable for YouTube, Google Play promo placement, websites, and landscape social placements. The video will present authentic BeatBox product screens in a premium mobile-device treatment, demonstrate the marketplace’s core value, and close with a clear brand call to action.

> **Recommended creative direction:** a caption-led product film with an original Afrobeat-inspired instrumental bed and no narration. This keeps the story understandable with sound off, avoids rushed voiceover, and works cleanly for store-review audiences.

## Inputs and constraints

| Area | Decision |
|---|---|
| Primary purpose | App-store feature / promotional video for BeatBox |
| Duration | 30 seconds |
| Output orientation | 16:9 landscape, 1920×1080 master |
| Language | English on-screen captions; no narration |
| Source material | The six user-supplied, authentic BeatBox mobile screenshots |
| Visual style | Premium dark-and-gold product film with a realistic black phone frame, restrained motion, soft gold light, and clean off-white/dark surroundings |
| Music | One original, royalty-safe Afrobeat-inspired instrumental; no sampled commercial music |
| Final call to action | **Discover. Stream. Create.** |
| Product accuracy | Use actual screenshots without fabricating app functionality, pricing, ratings, downloads, or platform approval claims |

## Production workflow

1. **Prepare source screens.** Preserve the supplied screenshot files as immutable source material, select the clearest crop of each screen, and place every vertical screen inside a consistent portrait phone mockup within a 16:9 frame. No app UI will be recreated or altered.

2. **Create visual references.** Before generating any video clips, create one upright 16:9 reference still that establishes the dark-and-gold product-film environment, black phone treatment, lighting, and background texture. Create any additional reference angles only from that primary reference, maintaining one consistent phone style.

3. **Create the original music bed.** Generate a single royalty-safe instrumental based on the sound blueprint: approximately 100 BPM, warm Afrobeat-influenced percussion, rounded bass, restrained marimba/synth texture, and no vocals. The track will be mixed once across all clips.

4. **Generate and assemble clip motion.** Produce six short, controlled 3–5 second product shots following the storyboard. Each motion prompt will explicitly preserve the device, the uploaded app screenshot within it, the dark/gold palette, and all product-screen readability. Clips will be created independently where scene cuts are intended, then edited into a single continuous story.

5. **Add minimal caption and brand treatment.** Place the approved English captions in a high-contrast, safe-title area outside the device frame. Use only the exact copy in the story table, the existing BeatBox brand treatment, and the final CTA. No rating requests, unverified superlatives, or store-approval claims will be included.

6. **Mix, export, and validate.** Export an H.264 MP4, 1920×1080, 30 seconds, with AAC stereo sound. Confirm the phone screens are upright and legible, captions remain inside 16:9 title-safe margins, the audio has no clipping, the file plays correctly, and the final frame holds long enough to read.

## Distribution notes

The execution phase will verify the current store-specific submission requirements before delivery. The plan intentionally produces a universal 16:9 master: if a listing requires a hosted promo-video URL rather than a direct video upload, the master can be uploaded to the required approved video host and linked from the store console. The project will not claim Google Play or App Store approval before an actual store review is complete.

## Acceptance checks

| Check | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Product truthfulness | Every shown product screen is one of the supplied authentic BeatBox screens; no invented feature, payment, rating, or installation claim appears |
| Brand consistency | Dark charcoal, gold, off-white, BeatBox logo, and one consistent phone mockup style are used from start to finish |
| Readability | Vertical UI, captions, and final CTA remain legible in a landscape player on mobile and desktop |
| Audio | Original, non-vocal instrumental is audible but does not overpower visual storytelling; no copyrighted track is inserted |
| Technical delivery | Upright 16:9 MP4 plays end-to-end at 1920×1080, with no black-frame, rotation, artifact, or clipping defect |

## Assumptions and risks

The supplied screenshots are treated as approved authentic product assets. The selected 30-second caption-led format is the recommended choice requested by the user. Exact store video-hosting, thumbnail, age-rating, and asset requirements can change; they will be verified against the relevant live publisher guidance immediately before submission. The final master is not a substitute for creating a signed Android release or passing any app-store review.
