# BeatBox Promotional Advertisement Blueprint

## Global creative definition

**Purpose:** Promote BeatBox as Liberia’s creator-first marketplace and social platform for discovering beats, licensing music, publishing content, connecting with producers, and using AI-assisted marketplace guidance.

**Audience:** Liberian and African artists, producers, vocalists, DJs, engineers, and music fans who need a trustworthy place to discover, preview, license, publish, and grow.

**Master format:** 30 seconds, vertical 9:16, optimized for TikTok, Facebook Reels, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. Keep important typography inside a centered safe area for platform UI overlays.

**Visual style:** Premium Afro-modern product commercial. Dark charcoal and near-black backgrounds; warm BeatBox gold/yellow accents; crisp white editorial typography; subtle grain; polished phone UI close-ups; vinyl-disc and waveform motifs; fast but controlled camera motion; high contrast; no third-party logos or copied social-platform branding.

**Recurring elements:**

| Element | Definition |
|---|---|
| BeatBox identity | Official BeatBox wordmark and provided logo asset; dark/gold/white palette |
| Phone interface | Modern smartphone with BeatBox screens, upright and readable, no fabricated user reviews or ratings |
| Music creator | Young Liberian/African producer in a compact home studio, dark tee or neutral streetwear, warm monitor glow |
| Listener/artist | Young Liberian/African vocalist or artist auditioning a beat on a phone, expressive but natural |
| Audio visualizer | Gold waveform and vinyl-disc geometry that responds to the music |

**Narrator:** Warm, confident Liberian-English male or female voice, medium pace, clear diction, aspirational but practical.

**Narration script:** “Your sound deserves more than a folder. Meet BeatBox — Liberia’s marketplace for original beats, clear licenses, and creators ready to move. Preview with confidence, keep masters private, and connect with the people building the next release. Discover your sound. Publish your work. Move with more ownership. Start on BeatBox today.”

**BGM:** Separate background track, energetic Afrobeat-inspired instrumental at approximately 104 BPM. Core palette: punchy kick, clean percussion, warm bass, bright marimba or pluck synth, muted guitar stabs, subtle Liberian hand-drum texture, tasteful vocal chop without words. Start sparse, build to full groove by 00:12, brief lift at 00:23, resolve with a clean branded hit at 00:29. No copyrighted melody or imitation of a named artist.

## Transition requirements

Every clip uses detailed motion continuity: subjects retain their appearance, movement follows a physically plausible path, interface elements stay upright, and existing objects do not pop in or disappear unexpectedly. Use scene cuts only between clips 1–2 and 3–4; use continuous motion within each clip. Typography should be added in post or generated only when legible; do not rely on AI-generated text for critical CTA accuracy.

## Platform adaptations

The master is vertical 9:16. Keep a 12% top and bottom safe margin for platform controls. A 16:9 adaptation can crop the hero phone and studio scenes around the central action, while preserving a centered logo and CTA. Recommended caption: “Liberia’s beat marketplace for original sound, clear licenses, and creators ready to move. Discover, publish, and move with more ownership at sastechorg-beatbox.vercel.app.”

## Reference images required before execution

1. Primary visual anchor: vertical premium smartphone product shot showing BeatBox’s dark/gold interface, abstract waveform, and vinyl-disc motif on a white background, no text or logos.
2. Producer studio reference: young Liberian/African music producer in a compact warm-lit home studio, phone in hand, vertical composition, no text or logos.
3. Artist listening reference: young Liberian/African vocalist or artist in a warm recording environment, phone and microphone present, vertical composition, no text or logos.
