# BeatBox Advertisement Posting Guide

## Master asset

The finished master is a 30-second vertical MP4 at 1080 × 1920 with H.264 video and AAC audio. It is suitable as a single source file for TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

## Recommended copy

**Caption:** Liberia’s beat marketplace for original sound, clear licenses, and creators ready to move. Discover, publish, and move with more ownership on BeatBox.

**Call to action:** Visit https://sastechorg-beatbox.vercel.app

**Suggested hashtags:** #BeatBox #LiberianMusic #AfricanMusic #MusicProducers #IndependentArtists #Afrobeats

## Upload notes

Keep the caption URL in the platform’s link field where available, and place the same URL in the first line of the caption when a direct link field is not available. Preserve the 9:16 master without adding letterboxing. The title-safe copy is kept away from the very top and bottom of the frame so platform controls do not cover the message.

For YouTube Shorts, use the title “BeatBox — Liberia’s Marketplace for Original Sound” and paste the URL into the description. For TikTok and Reels, use the caption above and pin a comment with the live site link if the platform limits clickable caption URLs.

## Production note

The daily AI video-shot quota allowed one fully generated motion shot. The final master therefore combines that generated opening shot with the approved generated reference stills, controlled animated camera moves, post-added legible overlays, and the separately generated original instrumental and narration. This fallback keeps the visual direction coherent and ensures the critical URL and CTA are exact rather than AI-generated approximations.
